# Meridian Analytics — Onboarding FAQ

A short FAQ for new workspace administrators.

## How do I create my first workspace?

Sign in to the Console, click **New Workspace**, choose a region, and name it. Remember
that the region cannot be changed later.

## How do I invite teammates?

Go to **Settings → Members → Invite**. Invitations expire after 7 days.

## Where do I find my API token?

Under **Settings → API Keys**. Only workspace administrators can view or create tokens.

## What happens if I lose access?

Contact another administrator on your workspace, or email `support@meridian.example`.
Meridian staff will never ask you to share your API token or password by email.
