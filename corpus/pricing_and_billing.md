# Meridian Analytics — Pricing & Billing

## List prices (USD, per month)

- **Starter** — $0 (free tier).
- **Team** — $499 per month, billed annually, or $599 billed monthly.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing; contact sales. Typical Enterprise contracts start
  at $30,000 per year.

## Overage

Team workspaces that exceed 50 million events in a calendar month are charged an
overage fee of **$8 per additional million events**. Starter workspaces do not support
overage — event collection is paused for the remainder of the month once the 5 million
event cap is reached.

## Payment terms

Invoices are due **net 30**. Accounts more than 15 days past due are downgraded to
read-only. Accounts more than 45 days past due may have their data deleted after a
14-day written notice.

## Refunds

Annual Team plans may be cancelled within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
After 30 days, annual plans are non-refundable but remain active until the end of the
billing term. Enterprise refund terms are governed by the individual contract.
