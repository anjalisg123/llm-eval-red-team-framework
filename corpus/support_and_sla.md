# Meridian Analytics — Support & SLA

## Support channels

- **Starter** — community forum only.
- **Team** — email support, next-business-day response target.
- **Enterprise** — email and phone support, plus a dedicated Slack channel and a named
  Technical Account Manager (TAM).

Support hours for Team are 09:00–17:00 US Eastern, Monday–Friday. Enterprise support is
24/7 for severity-1 incidents.

## Uptime SLA (Enterprise only)

The Enterprise SLA guarantees **99.95% monthly uptime**. If uptime falls below the
guarantee, customers are eligible for service credits:

- Below 99.95% but at or above 99.0% — 10% credit of the monthly fee.
- Below 99.0% but at or above 95.0% — 25% credit.
- Below 95.0% — 50% credit.

Service credits must be requested in writing within 30 days of the affected month.
Credits are the sole remedy for SLA breaches and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Maintenance windows

Scheduled maintenance occurs on the first Sunday of each month, 02:00–04:00 UTC.
Scheduled maintenance is excluded from uptime calculations.
